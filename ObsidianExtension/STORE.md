Browse your Obsidian **Vaults** folder by folder, then open a note in Obsidian or use **Copy Obsidian URI** to get a link to it. To search notes across every vault, turn on **Notes** in Tuna's Sources settings.

On a vault you can **Open Vault in Obsidian**, **Search Vault**, or create a **New Note**. Select or type some text and run **New Obsidian Note** to capture it. **Open Daily Note** opens today's daily note in the vault you used last. Select Obsidian in Tuna to search or start a new note.

You need Obsidian installed with at least one vault.
