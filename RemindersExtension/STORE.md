Search your reminders from Tuna, then **Mark Complete** or **Open in Reminders**. Select or type some text and run **Create Reminder** to add it, or **Add to Reminders List** to pick the list.

Select Reminders in Tuna and browse it to see your reminders.

macOS asks for access to your reminders the first time you use the extension.
