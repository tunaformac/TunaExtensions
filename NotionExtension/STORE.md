Browse **Notion** for recently edited pages and databases, or type to search across your connected workspaces. Turn on **Pages** to add your top-level pages to Tuna's global search.

Select Notion in Tuna and browse it to get to the same places.

Connect one or more Notion workspaces in the extension's settings. Tuna only sees the pages and databases you share with it during the connection.
