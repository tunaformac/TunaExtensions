Turn Philips Hue rooms and zones on or off directly from Tuna. Search for a room by name, press Tab, and choose **Turn On** or **Turn Off**. If a light is offline, use **Check Connection** to refresh its status.

To get started, run **Connect First Hue Bridge** and press the physical link button when prompted. You can also enter bridge details manually and connect multiple bridges.

Requires a Philips Hue Bridge on the same network as your Mac. Automatic discovery uses Philips Hue’s discovery service. Light control goes directly to your bridge, and application keys are stored in macOS Keychain.
