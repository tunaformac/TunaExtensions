Make memes without leaving Tuna. Open **Make Meme** to browse templates as a thumbnail grid, pick one, and type each box's text separated by `|`, or just press Enter to use the template's example text. The finished image becomes your selection, ready to paste, copy, share, or preview.

It works right away with Memegen's free templates. To also use Imgflip's 100 most popular templates, add your Imgflip username and password in the extension's settings. The password is stored in your Mac Keychain.

**Add to Favorites** keeps the templates you use most at the top. Template lists are cached so they load instantly and keep working offline.
