Browse **Poof Snippets** to search your snippets by description, trigger, or text, and paste one. Date, time, UUID, and clipboard placeholders are filled in when you paste.

Select or type some text and run **Create Snippet** to save it as a new snippet with a trigger of your choice. **Edit Snippet** opens the snippet's file in your editor, and **Delete Snippet** removes it. Other entries in the same file stay as they are.

Snippets stay out of Tuna's global search until you turn the ones you want on. The extension uses Poof's own snippet folder, including a custom location. Pasting from Tuna removes the cursor placeholder but can't place the cursor the way Poof does.
