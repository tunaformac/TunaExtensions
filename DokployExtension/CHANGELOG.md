# Dokploy Extension Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Search Dokploy applications and compose services across one or more servers.
- Browse Dokploy > project > service.
- Deploy, redeploy, start, and stop (with confirmation) services; open them in the Dokploy panel;
  open or copy their domain.
- Requires Tuna 0.95 and TunaKit 1.21.0.
