Browse **Homebrew** and type to find formulae and casks, then install, uninstall, or upgrade them, or open their info page, without opening a terminal.

The **Homebrew** item also gives you:

- **Browse Installed** and **Browse Outdated** to see what's on your Mac
- **Install ...** and **Install cask ...** for packages you name directly
- **Update**, **Upgrade**, and **Cleanup** for the whole installation

Commands run in the background through your own Homebrew, which downloads from the internet as usual. Homebrew must be installed. Tuna finds `brew` on its own, or you can set a custom path in the extension's settings.
