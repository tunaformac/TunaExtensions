Browse **Recent 2FA Codes** to see verification codes from messages you received recently, then paste or copy one with Tuna's usual text actions. You can also select Messages in Tuna and browse it.

Only the code, sender, and time are shown. The rest of the message stays hidden. Codes never show up in Tuna's global search, and nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

Tuna needs Full Disk Access in System Settings to read your messages. In the settings you can choose how far back to look, from 5 minutes to an hour, and whether to skip messages you've already read.
