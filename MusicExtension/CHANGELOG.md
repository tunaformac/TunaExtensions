# Music Extension Changelog

## 0.1

- Now Playing plus Play/Pause, Next Track, Previous Track, Toggle Shuffle, and Toggle Repeat.
- Songs, Albums, Artists, and Playlists browse roots for the Music library; library items stay out of global search until opted in.
- Play, Shuffle, Show in Music, Favorite, and Add to Playlist actions through Music.app.
- Apple Music search with Recently Played, For You, artwork, and Open in Music via MusicKit.
- Requires Tuna 0.95 / TunaKit 1.21.0.
