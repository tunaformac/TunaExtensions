Select Music in Tuna and browse it for **Now Playing & Controls**: play/pause, next and previous track, shuffle, and repeat. Browse your **Songs**, **Albums**, **Artists**, and **Playlists**, then **Play**, **Shuffle**, **Show in Music**, **Favorite**, or **Add to Playlist**. **Apple Music** searches the Apple Music catalog as you type, and shows Recently Played and For You when the search is empty. **Open in Music** opens a result in Music.

Everything plays in Music. Playing an album or artist fills a playlist named Tuna in your library and plays it.

macOS asks for permission for Tuna to control Music the first time you use it. Apple Music search asks for Media & Apple Music access and talks to Apple's servers. Library items stay out of global search unless you turn them on in Tuna's Sources settings.
