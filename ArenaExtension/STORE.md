Browse your Are.na channels and their blocks right from Tuna. **Open on Are.na** takes you to a channel or block on the web, and **Resolve** turns a block into its underlying text, URL, image, or attachment so you can keep acting on it. Images and attachments are downloaded first.

Use **Save to Are.na** on a URL, some text, an image file, or a clipboard image to add it to a channel you pick.

Connect your Are.na account in the extension's settings. Tuna asks for read and write access so it can both browse and save.
