Search your Safari **Bookmarks**, **Favorites**, and **Reading List** from Tuna, then use **Open in Safari** or **Copy URL**.

Select Safari in Tuna and browse it to get to your bookmarks, favorites, and reading list.

**Safari Utilities** adds **Current Page**, the frontmost tab's address to open or copy, plus **New Tab** and **Last Download**, the newest file in your Downloads folder. They stay out of global search until you set Safari Utilities to **All** under **In global search** in Tuna's Sources settings.

Bookmarks are read from Safari's own files, which macOS protects, so Tuna needs Full Disk Access in System Settings. Tuna checks Safari's frontmost tab whenever it refreshes its sources, so macOS can ask for permission to control Safari as soon as Tuna starts with Safari open.
