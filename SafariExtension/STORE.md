Search your Safari **Bookmarks**, **Favorites**, and **Reading List** from Tuna, then use **Open in Safari** or **Copy URL**. **Current Page** gives you the frontmost tab's address to open or copy. **New Tab** and **New Private Window** open Safari windows, and **Last Download** gives you the newest file in your Downloads folder.

Select Safari in Tuna and browse it to get to your bookmarks, favorites, and reading list.

Bookmarks are read from Safari's own files, which macOS protects, so Tuna needs Full Disk Access in System Settings. macOS also asks for permission to control Safari the first time you use **Current Page** or open a new window.
