Browse **mymind** to see your library, newest first, and type to search it. **mymind Spaces** lets you open each Space. **Resolve** turns an object into its original URL, note text, or file. **Open Original** opens its source, and Quick Look previews it.

Use **Save to mymind** on a URL, text, an image, a PDF, or another supported file, or **Save to mymind space** to choose a Space for it. You get a link to the saved object when it's done.

You need a mymind access key. Create one in mymind's extension settings, then enter its Key ID and Private Key in Tuna. The private key is stored in your Mac Keychain. Choose Read only for browsing and search, or Full access to save. Requests count against your mymind credits.
