Search your Chrome bookmarks across every profile, or open a profile directly. **Open in Google Chrome** and **Copy URL** work on any bookmark.

Select Google Chrome in Tuna and browse it to get to its **Bookmarks** and **Profiles**.

**Chrome Utilities** adds **Current Page**, the frontmost tab's address to open or copy elsewhere, plus **New Chrome Window** and **New Incognito Window**. They stay out of global search until you set Chrome Utilities to **All** under **In global search** in Tuna's Sources settings.

Bookmarks and profiles are read from Chrome's own files, and nothing in Chrome is changed. **Current Page** asks macOS for permission to control Chrome the first time you use it.
