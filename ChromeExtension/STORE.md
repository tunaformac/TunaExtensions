Search your Chrome bookmarks across every profile, open a profile directly, or start a **New Chrome Window** or **New Incognito Window**. **Current Page** gives you the frontmost tab's address to open or copy elsewhere. **Open in Google Chrome** and **Copy URL** work on any bookmark.

Select Google Chrome in Tuna and browse it to get to its **Bookmarks** and **Profiles**.

Bookmarks and profiles are read from Chrome's own files, and nothing in Chrome is changed. **Current Page** asks macOS for permission to control Chrome the first time you use it. Incognito tabs are never indexed.
