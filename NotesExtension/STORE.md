Search your Apple Notes from Tuna and open any note with **Open in Notes**. Select or type some text and run **Create Note** to turn it into a new note. The first line becomes the title.

Select Notes in Tuna and browse it to see all your notes.

Tuna needs Full Disk Access in System Settings to read your notes, and macOS asks for permission to control Notes the first time you create one.
