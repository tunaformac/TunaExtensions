Select files or images and run **Cloud Share** to upload them to your own Amazon S3 or S3-compatible bucket. The public link goes on the clipboard, one per line for several files. **Cloud Share to Shelf** uploads in the background and puts the links on Shelf, so large uploads don't hold you up. With more than one destination, **Cloud Share to…** and **Cloud Share to Shelf via…** let you choose where the files go.

Browse **Cloud Shared** to search what's in the bucket, newest first. Open or copy a link, or remove a file with **Delete from Cloud**.

Add a destination in the extension's settings with your bucket, region, and access keys. The secret key is stored in your Mac Keychain. **Test Destination** checks the setup from start to finish. You can set a path prefix, a filename template, and a custom public URL. Nothing is uploaded or listed until you run an action or browse.
